Camping with Rillas

One day, Theotokis said to Aristotelis, "Hey, want to go camping sometime?" Aristotelis replied, "We'll freeze to death!" "Come on, let's go. Do we have tents?" "No." "Sleeping bags to sleep in?" "No." "Flashlights?" "No." "So what do we actually have?" "Nothing." "Then we need to go buy some stuff!" "Okay, okay."

They hopped on their bikes — vroom, vroom, vroom! — and rode to the store. They bought everything: tents, sleeping bags, flashlights, and everything they'd need to cook. They even arranged to leave early Friday morning, so they'd have three whole days — Friday, Saturday, and Sunday — for their trip.

One question remained: "Where should we go camping?" "Let's find Rillas and the monkey — they camp in the wild all the time, they can show us a good spot." "Great idea, let's go!"

They found Rillas. "Hey, Rillas!" "What can I do for you, guys?" "We want to go camping." Rillas laughed. "Camping? But you're humans — we live in nature every single day! If you start a fire in the jungle, we'll all be in trouble!" Then he turned serious. "Alright, we'll find a spot near a lake. Do you want Rilaki to come too?" "Yes, yes!" "Let's tell the monkey to bring Dakis and Akis along."

They went to the monkeys' house. "Little monkey! Hey, little monkey!" Dakis popped out. "What can I do for you, guys?" "We want to go camping!" "I'll go get my dad." Maimudakis came out. "What can I do for you, guys?" Rillas explained, "They want to sleep outside, in nature!" "Seriously? Theotokis and Aristotelis?" "Yes, they want to give it a try." "Alright, alright, let's go."

They decided to pick a spot where lots of animals lived — maybe even a tiger or two — to see if their two friends could really handle camping. So off they all went: Maimudakis, Dakis, Akis, Rilaki, Rillas, Theotokis, and Aristotelis. They found a beautiful, lush green spot right by the lake — that's where they'd set up camp.

"Should we pitch our tents?" asked Theotokis. "You don't even know how," teased Rillas. Theotokis tried, but his tent kept collapsing, again and again. Aristotelis, meanwhile, had his own tent up in no time. "Need a hand?" "No, I've got it!" A little later, though: "Okay, fine, a little help would be nice." Together they finished both tents and tucked their sleeping bags and flashlights inside.

As night fell, they asked, "Should we light a fire and cook something?" "We'll roast you some bananas on the coals," Rillas suggested. The two friends had never heard of such a thing, but they happily agreed. Rillas brought back an armful of bananas, they lit the fire, ate their fill under the stars, and went off to sleep.

Late that night, Rilaki secretly woke up Dakis and Akis. "I want to play a prank on Aristotelis and Theotokis!" "What kind of prank?" "Let's make animal noises and scare them a little." They crept outside and started: "Hoo-hoo! Hoo-hoo!" Theotokis woke up terrified and rushed to Aristotelis's tent. "Do you hear that?" "I don't hear anything." The noises kept going. "I don't know... let's sleep together, I'm a little scared!" "Okay."

A little while later came a deep "Roooar! Roooar!" Certain it was a tiger, they panicked and ran to wake up Rillas. Meanwhile, Rilaki, Dakis, and Akis had already slipped back into their own tents. Rillas looked around. "Guys, there's nothing here." "No, no — come sleep with us, we heard it clearly!" "Okay, okay."

The next morning, Rillas, Theotokis, and Aristotelis woke up exhausted. Rilaki, Dakis, and Akis couldn't stop giggling. "What's so funny?" "Nothing, nothing." Still, everyone had a wonderful time out in the wild. Little by little, Rillas and Maimudakis began to suspect the kids were behind it all, since Theotokis and Aristotelis had been so badly spooked.

Later, they asked Theotokis and Aristotelis, "What did you hear last night?" "Something like... hoo-hoo! Roooar!" So they called over Dakis, Akis, and Rilaki. "Did you pull a prank?" "We did." "Then we have to get you back! Tonight, we'll wait for you to fall asleep, then we'll hide — and when you wake up looking for us, we won't be anywhere to be found." "Okay, okay."

That evening, Maimudakis asked the kids, "Are you three sleeping together tonight?" "Yes! Sleepover! It's going to be amazing!" As soon as the kids drifted off, Rillas woke up Theotokis, Aristotelis, and Maimudakis. "Let's go do the prank!" They climbed high up into a tree and started dropping little pebbles onto the kids' tent.

Dakis woke up first. "Something's happening — it's raining!" "It's summer, it can't be raining," said Akis. They went outside to check, but there was nothing there. The moment they climbed back in — tap, tap, tap! "Let's go wake up our dads!" They ran from tent to tent — all empty! "We're all alone! Help, help!" All three kids burst into tears.

Just then, Rillas dropped down from the tree. "Boo!" The kids were scared out of their wits. "Now tell me — did you bother Theotokis and Aristotelis last night?" "Yes... we learned our lesson, we won't do it again." "Are you sure?" Dakis and Akis snickered quietly. "Yes, yes..." Rillas turned to Theotokis and Aristotelis. "That wasn't right of them — it was your first time camping, and we scared you. We're sorry." "It's okay," they said, smiling.

Everyone had a wonderful weekend together. On Sunday morning, they hopped back on their bikes — vroom, vroom, vroom! — and rode home, full of brand-new memories from their very first camping trip.
