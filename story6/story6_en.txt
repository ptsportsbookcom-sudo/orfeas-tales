Duke Kaboom

One afternoon, Theotokis and Aristotelis were sitting in front of the TV when they saw something that made them jump off the sofa.

"And now, ladies and gentlemen!" the presenter was saying. "The world's greatest stuntman is coming to Cyprus! Duke Kaboom!"

"Duke Kaboom!" yelled Aristotelis. "We have to go and see him!"

Rillas looked up from his bananas. "Boom? What boom? Like a motorcycle?"

"No, Rillas," said Theotokis. "It's not a boom. It's Duke Kaboom. That's his name."

"Ah. Boom. Boom," said Rillas, and went back to his bananas.

They went to the ticket booth.

"Three tickets, please," said Aristotelis.

The cashier looked past Aristotelis, spotted Rillas, and blinked. "Three? That one there... is a gorilla."

"Yes," said Aristotelis calmly.

"And how exactly is he getting in?"

"He'll wear a hat."

Rillas put on an enormous hat and a long coat, and somehow, he walked straight past the ticket inspector without any trouble at all. They hopped on their quad bikes and rode to the arena.

The place was packed. The lights went down. The music started.

"Ladies and gentlemen!" announced the presenter. "We are proud to present — Mister Duke Kaboom!"

The crowd went wild. "Duke Kaboom! Duke Kaboom!"

"The first stunt!" declared the presenter. "Mister Duke Kaboom will fly through three rings of fire and perform a full somersault on his motorcycle!"

Duke Kaboom revved up. Boom! Boom! Boom! He blasted through the first ring, the second, the third — and did one, two, three, ten somersaults in the air — and landed perfectly!

"Duke Kaboom! Duke Kaboom!"

Rillas was clapping so hard the entire row was shaking.

"The second stunt!" announced the presenter. "Mister Duke Kaboom will jump higher than any human has ever jumped — and land without a single problem!"

Duke Kaboom revved again. Boom! Boom! Boom! He shot up so high that Rillas's jaw dropped — and he landed as softly as a feather.

"Duke Kaboom! Duke Kaboom!"

"And now," said the presenter, drawing out every word, "the final and most spectacular stunt. Mister Duke Kaboom will ride his motorcycle and fly over one hundred trucks!"

"One hundred trucks!" whispered Theotokis.

The crowd fell silent. Everyone waited.

A minute passed. Then another.

"Ladies and gentlemen," the presenter said, his voice cracking, "unfortunately... we will not be performing our final stunt. Mister Duke Kaboom's motorcycle has a burst tyre."

The crowd groaned.

"No!" shouted Aristotelis. He turned to Theotokis. "We have two quad bikes outside!"

Theotokis looked at him. "Aristotelis... don't."

But Aristotelis had already raised his hand high. "Sir! Sir! We have two quad bikes!"

The presenter stared at him. "There are quad bikes in Cyprus?"

"Yes! And they're right here!"

Duke Kaboom climbed down from the stage. He was tall, wearing a leather suit, carrying his helmet. He looked at Aristotelis, then at Theotokis — and then he saw Rillas.

"Oh my goodness! Oh my goodness! What is that creature?!"

"That's Rillas," said Aristotelis. "Our friend. He's a bit shy."

Rillas turned red and looked away.

Duke Kaboom walked over to the quad bikes. He looked them over from front to back. He tapped them. He whistled. "These are magnificent! I'll do the stunt with them!"

They lined up one hundred trucks in the middle of the arena.

Rillas — who needed no convincing when quad bikes were involved — jumped on his and started doing warm-up laps, grinning from ear to ear.

Duke Kaboom climbed onto Aristotelis's quad bike, built up speed, and launched himself into the air.

Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!

He flew — one truck, ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety, a hundred —

He kept going.

One hundred and ten. One hundred and twenty. One hundred and thirty. One hundred and forty.

He landed perfectly.

"DUKE KABOOM! DUKE KABOOM! ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY TRUCKS!"

It was a world record.

Duke Kaboom took a bow and pointed straight at the boys. "Thank you, ladies and gentlemen! And a warm round of applause for Theotokis and Aristotelis, who would not let this show die!"

Backstage, Duke Kaboom was waiting for them with a huge smile.

"What do you want, kids? Anything you like!"

"Bananas," said Rillas quietly. It was the first thing he had said all evening.

Duke Kaboom burst out laughing. "Ten bananas for my gorilla friend! Right away!"

Aristotelis received a helmet signed by Duke Kaboom. Theotokis received a signed cape — the very one Duke Kaboom had worn during the show. And Rillas ate his bananas one by one, perfectly happy.

"One day," said Duke Kaboom, "I'll call you to come to America."

"OK, OK," said Aristotelis, very calmly — as if it were the most ordinary thing in the world.

They were all happy. Theotokis with his cape, Aristotelis with his helmet, and Rillas — with ten bananas in his arms.

Best kids!
