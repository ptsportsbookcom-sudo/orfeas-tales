## Interpretations made in the text — all CONFIRMED by Pantelis 2026-10-08
1. The first trip back to the door is just Rilaki + Dakis + Akis ("ο φίλος τους ο Ρίλλας" read as Rilaki). Rillas and Maimudakis only come on the second trip, so the group is 7, matching Story 22.
2. The riddle wording is tightened to "When you say my name, I disappear. What am I?" (answer: silence / η σιγή).
3. The bus riddle is answered by **Rilaki: "I'm almost six!"**. The "Who's the driver, the monster?" line is his too, and Theotokis corrects him.
4. Theotokis's garbled first clue ("λιοντάρια… λείπουν… σερνόταν") is written as drag marks: "something big was dragging itself along here".
5. **The ash is explained** by the Dragon: the pain made a burst of fire come out of him. This is my addition to close the clue.
6. "βολτίτσα με τα πόδια" is written as "a little flight". "δεν πάω μακριά" is read as "I can't go far" (because he's hurt).
7. "Ρε θείε" is dropped. Rillas's "μικρέ Ρίλλα" is kept as a nickname for his son.
8. "Μπορώ να φτιάξω άλλο δράκο… Δεν είναι η ώρα τώρα" at the end is dropped as off-story chatter.
9. Rilaki apologises for not visiting in a while, which ties to the Dragon's Story 22 fear that they'd "lie and never come back".
