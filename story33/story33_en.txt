The Missing Bride

Once upon a time, Arxigeas and Arxigeena were planning their wedding. Arxigeena was from Planet 650, and Arxigeas was from the planet Aphrodite.

"We have to invite our friends too!" they said.

So Arxigeas phoned Theotokis. Ring ring... ring ring...

"Oh my goodness!" said Theotokis. "It's Arxigeas calling us! Hey, friend, what's going on?"

"Theotokis! We want to invite you to our wedding!"

"What did you say?"

"We're finally getting married, and we want all of you to come!"

"But who exactly?"

"You, Aristotelis, Rillas, Rilaki, Rilena, Maimudakis, Akis, Dakis and Maimudakena."

"Everyone?"

"Yes, everyone!"

"Thank you very much! And when is the wedding?"

"Next Sunday."

"Okay, okay!"

So the boys phoned everyone, and of course everyone said, "We're coming! No question about it!"

Then Theotokis called Arxigeas back. "Okay, friend. I think the spaceship you left us can fit all of us."

"Great! But come early, on Friday. We'll have a big party before the wedding!"

"Okay, okay! We'll stay until Sunday."

Then the phone rang again. It was Rillas. "Hey, are there bananas? I'm not coming if there are no bananas!"

"Oh my goodness," laughed Theotokis. "Don't worry, we'll bring you bananas!"

On Friday, the boys jumped on their quad bikes with crates of bananas. Brrrm, brrrm... they rode to the jungle, where the spaceship was hidden under the trees.

The spaceship was small, so Theotokis used his gadgets to make it big, big enough for everyone. Then they all climbed aboard.

"I'll fly us there!" said Aristotelis.

"Don't even think about it!" said Theotokis. "Autopilot. I'll take care of it."

Beep, beep... plilililili. He typed in the right directions. "Three, two, one... blast off!" Psssss! Tsssss! And off they went!

Space was beautiful. They flew past Neptune, Saturn and Uranus... and on Saturday they landed on Aphrodite.

Arxigeas was very nervous. He was running here and there. "Oh dear, the wedding must be perfect, and Arxigeena..."

"Hey, where is Arxigeena?" asked the boys.

"Ah! Arxigeena told me I mustn't see her until we get married. She's on Planet 650. The groom must not see the bride before the wedding day. That's the tradition."

"So we won't see her today. Okay, okay!"

On Saturday they played, they ate, they collected sparkling diamonds, and they did somersaults with Arxigeas. Then they all went to sleep.

The wedding was on Sunday at three o'clock in the afternoon. In the morning everyone woke up happy and excited. They set up a big party with music and lots of food.

Arxigeena was supposed to arrive at eleven, so that Rilena and Maimudakena could help her get ready.

Twelve o'clock came... "Where is Arxigeena?"

One o'clock came. Arxigeas started sweating. "Oh dear, what if she changed her mind? But I can't phone her now, or she'll know I'm nervous!"

Two o'clock came. Arxigeas was soaked with sweat! He paced up and down. "Oh no, she changed her mind and she's not coming!"

"Just call her!" said Theotokis. "Let's see if everything is okay."

"No, no, no, no!" said Arxigeas. "It's over, it's over."

Three o'clock came, the time of the wedding. And Arxigeas burst into tears. "She changed her mind... boohoo, boohoo..."

"Should I call her?" asked Theotokis. Ring ring... ring ring... nothing. He tried again. Ring ring... nothing.

"Could something have happened to her?"

"Oh no, now I'm even more worried! Do you think something happened?"

"I don't know. Is her planet far from here?"

"No, it's about thirty minutes by spaceship." Arxigeas stood up straight. "Men, attention! Everyone into the spaceship! We're going to find Arxigeena. Rilena and Maimudakena, you stay here, in case she arrives."

"Okay, okay!"

They flew to Planet 650. They searched here, they searched there. But the people there said, "She left this morning! She was very happy. She put on her wedding dress and she looked beautiful."

"Oh no," said Arxigeas. "Something has happened. Something has happened!"

"Is there a way to track her spaceship?"

"There is," said Theotokis. "I'll use everything I know to make a GPS, to find her from her spaceship's signal."

He worked for half an hour. Dilililili... he typed in the coordinates. Her spaceship was parked far away... in the middle of nowhere!

"Let's go find her!"

They flew there as fast as they could. They found the spaceship and called her on the radio. "Arxigeena, Arxigeena, are you okay?" No answer. "Arxigeena, are you okay?" Nothing.

They flew closer... and through the window they saw her, fainted, in her wedding dress, inside her spaceship.

"We have to get inside! But Arxigeena's spaceship has the latest defence technology. Nobody can just walk in!"

"Arxigeas, think!" said the boys. "What if she's hurt?"

He thought, and thought, and thought... "I've got it! We'll tow it to Neptune... I mean, to Aphrodite, where my house is. We'll sort it out there."

"Okay, okay!"

They tied ropes between the spaceships and slowly towed hers all the way to Aphrodite. By then it was five or six o'clock in the evening.

They went to open the door... Zzzzzz! The spaceship's defence system was full of electricity, and it zapped anyone who touched it!

"Theotokis, think!" said everyone. "Use your head!"

Theotokis thought and thought... "I've got it! Arxigeas, do you have a machine that cuts off electricity? So we can switch off the defence without burning out the battery?"

"Yes, yes, I have one!"

He brought it. BZZZT! The defence switched off, the door opened, and there was Arxigeena. She had bumped her head.

"Quick, let's take her to the hospital," said Arxigeas. "Never mind the wedding. It's okay."

At the hospital, the doctor said, "It's nothing. She just bumped her head."

Arxigeena woke up. "What's happening? Did the wedding happen?"

"What wedding?" said Arxigeas. "You... you... you fainted!"

"Oh, I'm so sorry! On my way here, there was a wave of meteorites. I couldn't avoid them, they crashed into my spaceship, and I bumped my head. I don't remember anything else... and now I see you."

"It doesn't matter, my love. It's late now. Everyone is going home. We'll get married another day."

"Are you crazy? Now! We're going to the church right now! Bring me the priest! I don't care if he's asleep! I'm getting married TODAY!"

"Okay, okay!"

Rilena and Maimudakena came and helped her get ready, and she looked beautiful again. The men decorated the church. And they brought the priest, a sleepy alien priest, because they had woken him up!

And at last, they got married.

"Arxigeas, do you take Arxigeena to be your wife forever?"

"Yes, yes!"

"Arxigeena, do you take Arxigeas to be your husband forever?"

"Yes, yes!"

"Then you may kiss the bride! You are husband and wife!"

"Perfect! Bravo!" everyone cheered.

Then they had a huge party, with music, food and dancing... until suddenly it was twelve o'clock at night!

"Oh no!" said Theotokis. "We're still here! What about school tomorrow? It's Monday!"

"Never mind," said Aristotelis. "We're having fun!"

But the boys are kids, and kids have to go to school... So this time, they missed just one day. After all, it's not every day your friends get married on another planet!

And they lived happily ever after, and we lived even better!

The End
