Rilaki Goes to the Moon

Once upon a time, Theotokis and Aristotelis heard that a rocket was about to launch for the moon. "Let's go watch!" said Aristotelis. "Let's bring Rillas and Maimudakis too — and their kids!" They called Rillas. "Do you want to come with Rilaki and watch a rocket launch to the moon?" "Wow, of course!" said Rillas. "Do you think Dakis and Akis will want to come?" asked Maimudakis. "Yes, yes, yes!" the boys shouted.

Everyone hopped on their quad bikes — vroom, vroom, vroom! — and set off. Along the way, Theotokis explained: "It's an experiment. The rocket will fly by itself, with no humans on board — it'll carry some fruit to the moon and bring it back, to see if it survives the trip." "Okay, okay!" everyone said.

They arrived at the launch site the next day. "Hide," Theotokis told them, "so the guards don't spot us and chase us off." They all ducked behind some crates and watched with wide eyes.

They watched the workers load the rocket with crates of fruit. "What's all this for?" Rilaki asked. "It's the fruit for the experiment," they explained. Suddenly Dakis counted them up: "Thirty crates... of bananas!" "Bananas?" Rilaki shouted, eyes lighting up. "They're taking bananas to the moon too?"

Before anyone could stop him, Rilaki slipped inside the rocket to grab a banana. Theotokis and Aristotelis spotted him just in time. "Rilaki got inside the rocket! Run!"

"We'll create a distraction," Theotokis said quickly. "Aristotelis and I will make a scene with the quad bikes to keep the guards busy. Rillas, Maimudakis, Dakis, Akis — go in, grab Rilaki, and get out fast!" "Okay, okay!"

Theotokis and Aristotelis zoomed around on their quad bikes shouting "Help, help!" and every guard came running toward them. At the same moment, Rillas, Maimudakis, Dakis, and Akis snuck into the rocket, searching for Rilaki.

They searched high, they searched low — and then came a loud "clack." The door had shut. And the countdown began: "Ten... nine... eight... seven... six... five... four... three... two... one... Liftoff!"

The rocket blasted off toward the moon — with Rillas, Maimudakis, Dakis, Akis, and Rilaki inside! They found five spacesuits — exactly enough — and, not knowing what else to do, sat down and ate the bananas.

At mission control, Theotokis and Aristotelis learned there were animals aboard the rocket. "Oh no! We have to bring them back safe and sound!" said Theotokis, worried sick.

The rocket landed on the moon. Rillas and Maimudakis put on the suits and stepped outside to see what this strange place was — and everyone else followed close behind.

Suddenly, they saw a shadow zip past them incredibly fast — "swoosh!" "It's nothing," they said, trying to reassure each other. "Just our imagination. We're alone up here, right?"

"Should we split up and search?" Rillas suggested. Rillas and Rilaki went one way; Maimudakis, Dakis, and Akis went the other.

Maimudakis, Dakis, and Akis found nothing at all. But as Rillas and Rilaki walked along, they tripped over something invisible. "Ow, ow!" And then a voice rang out: "Stop hitting my spaceship!"

Startled, they called for the others. "Help, help, come here!" Everyone came running — and then a creature pressed a button, making its spaceship visible. It was the leader of the aliens — the Archigeas.

"Who are you?" the Archigeas asked. "Did you come to destroy the moon? Are we enemies?" "No, no!" said Rillas. "We're animals from Earth — gorillas and monkeys. There are humans on Earth too, a bit smarter than us, but they damage the environment. We're the very best friends of two human kids, Theotokis and Aristotelis."

"I'm the Archigeas," said the alien. "I come from the planet Aphrodite. I came here to find diamonds — we eat them." "Did you find any?" Rilaki asked. "Plenty. I'm heading home to eat, because I'm starving." "Do you want to be friends?" Rilaki asked. "Friends? What's that?" They explained, and invited him to visit Earth secretly one day — "because if humans ever saw you, they'd want to catch you and run tests on you." "I already have lots of alien friends," the Archigeas said with a grin, "but why don't you stay and play with me a while first — and then I'll take you back to Earth. Secretly, of course!"

They played for hours in the moon's low gravity — jumping high, doing somersaults, playing hide-and-seek. The Archigeas showed them his diamonds, how he ate them, and gave them a full tour of his spaceship.

When they realized Theotokis and Aristotelis would be worried, they decided to head back to Earth. The Archigeas shrank his spaceship down and tucked it into his pocket. "Climb in — I'll take you to Earth, and then we'll come back here together."

As they got closer to Earth, they grew nervous. "If you land near people, they'll see the spaceship's lights!" said Rilaki. "Take us to the jungle instead — it's safe there." They set the coordinates and flew toward the jungle.

The Archigeas landed quietly among the trees. "Thank you for saving us," they all told him. "We're friends for life!" "It was nothing," the Archigeas replied, handing them a radio. "Take this so we can talk. I have two more spaceships — come visit me whenever you like." And with that, he flew off and vanished into the sky.

They called Theotokis and Aristotelis. "We're okay! Tell them to send someone to pick up the rocket they left on the moon. And you won't believe it — we made friends with the best alien in the whole world, the Archigeas, and soon you'll get to meet him too!" "Now we're off to sleep — we're exhausted." And they all lived happily ever after. Better than ever, really!
