The Diamonds of Aphrodite

Once upon a time, Arxigeena woke up and decided to go shopping on Aphrodite.

"Off you go," Arxigeas told her. "When you come home, I'll be right here."

"Okay, okay!"

So Arxigeena went shopping. She went here, she went there, and she bought all sorts of things.

But when she came home, Orfeas, everything was a mess. The door was broken and things were scattered everywhere.

"Arxigeas! Arxigeas!" she shouted. Nothing.

She ran into the bedroom. Nothing.

Only the babies were there, Geas and Eas, and they were crying. But they were far too little to tell her what had happened.

"Arxigeas! Arxigeas!" He was nowhere.

Then she looked here and there, and she found a note.

"Ha ha!" said the note. "It is I, the greatest villain in the whole world, Kakeas! I have kidnapped Arxigeas. To get him back, I want all the diamonds of Aphrodite. You have two weeks to find them, and I will contact you. And don't call the police, or you will never see Arxigeas again!"

"Oh my goodness!" said Arxigeena. "It's that Kakeas again! What am I going to do? What am I going to do?"

"I'll call our friends on Earth, Theotokis and Aristotelis."

She called them on the phone. Ring ring... ring ring...

"Hello? Theotokis here. Hey, Arxigeas!"

"No, it's not Arxigeas. It's me, Arxigeena."

"Arxigeena! What's going on?"

"You won't believe it! Kakeas came and kidnapped Arxigeas! Luckily he didn't take the babies too. And he wants all the diamonds of Aphrodite!"

"He wants what?"

"All the diamonds of Aphrodite!"

"Don't worry," Theotokis told her. "I'll get Aristotelis, Rillas and Rilaki, and we'll jump in the spaceship. In one week we'll be there. Try to stay calm. Don't worry about anything, we'll sort it out."

The boys jumped on their quad bikes, brrrm, brrrm, brrrm, and raced to the jungle.

There they found Rillas, lying down and relaxing.

"Rillas! Emergency, emergency!"

"What happened, boys? Oh my goodness, oh my goodness, what happened?"

"Kakeas has kidnapped our friend Arxigeas, and he's asking for something completely crazy!"

"What is he asking for? A hundred kilos of bananas?"

That Rillas and his bananas!

"No! All the diamonds of Aphrodite!"

"What? How is that even possible?!"

"Get Rilaki! We're getting on the rocket and leaving. Come on!"

Rillas got Rilaki and told Rilena they were going to help their friends. "Of course, of course!" she said.

"3, 2, 1... blast off!" Whoooosh!

On the way, Theotokis kept thinking. "What are we going to do? We have to do something. All the diamonds of Aphrodite... that's a bit difficult."

A week later, they landed on Aphrodite.

"Arxigeena! Is there any news?"

"He sent another message today. One week has already gone by. We only have one week left to gather all the diamonds."

"Did he tell you where to meet him?"

"No. He said he'll get in touch one day before."

"Okay," said Theotokis. "So we have one week to come up with the smartest plan ever. Because getting all the diamonds of Aphrodite is pretty much impossible."

Theotokis went to the libraries and read and read. One day passed. Two days passed.

Meanwhile, Aristotelis was making plans and practising with Rilaki. They also helped look after Geas and Eas, because Arxigeena was very worried.

Then one day, Theotokis found a book called "Alchemy: How to Make Temporary Diamonds from Stones".

"Oh, wow! Interesting," he said. "Let's read it."

The book had magic potions, Orfeas. With them, you could turn as many stones as you wanted into diamonds. But only for two days! After that, the diamonds would crumble and turn into sand.

Theotokis gathered Rillas, Rilaki and Aristotelis. "Guys, Arxigeena will look after Geas and Eas. I need you to find me all these ingredients."

"What do you need, exactly?"

"I need 1,000 kilos of sand."

"Oh my goodness," said Rillas. "Okay."

"I need lizard spit. I need water from the river of Aphrodite. Six guinea fowl feathers, and if there are no guinea fowl here, find something like them on Aphrodite. I need three hairs from the Wise Man of Aphrodite. Two eggs from alien birds. And one hundred real diamonds."

"Okay, okay!"

Everyone ran here and there. The easy things they found quickly, Orfeas.

But the two alien bird eggs and the three hairs were really hard. Where were they going to find the smartest man on Aphrodite?

Then, by chance, word got around that Kakeas had kidnapped Arxigeas, and everybody came to help. The whole village gathered! They found the Wise Man, and they found the eggs too.

They brought everything to Theotokis.

"Right," he said. "Leave me alone until tomorrow. I don't want anyone to bother me. I have something very important to do. I'm going to make a potion! I'm going to make a potion! Leave me alone! Leave me alone!"

By now, only two days were left.

Theotokis worked all night long. He did experiments, he tried again and again, but it didn't work, and he got very stressed.

"Oh no, we're running out of time! I only have 12 hours left!"

But somehow, in the end, he got the potion just right.

Meanwhile, Kakeas contacted Arxigeena. "Tomorrow at 12 o'clock I will come to the square of Aphrodite with Arxigeas. If I don't get the diamonds, you will never see him again!"

Theotokis gathered the whole village. They dropped grains of sand into the potion, and they turned into diamonds, Orfeas!

"But don't tell anyone that they'll crumble in two days!" Theotokis told everyone. "No, no!"

In the end, he made a whole mountain of diamonds. They were fake, of course, but you couldn't tell. They looked just like real ones.

They piled them up right in the middle of the village.

Then Kakeas's gigantic spaceship landed. Kakeas stepped out, and his guards were holding Arxigeas tight.

"Ha ha! I see you have gathered all the diamonds of Aphrodite! Guards! Guards! Come and take them all! Now I'm super rich! I have tons of money and diamonds!"

"Put them in the spaceship, please. But I won't let Arxigeas go until the very last diamond is on board and I'm sure they are real."

He had even brought a diamond expert. The expert looked at the diamonds from this side and from that side.

Theotokis got very nervous. "Oh my goodness, imagine if he realises they're fake!"

The expert looked and looked, and then he said, "Perfect! Alpha quality! The best of the best! Splendid!"

"Phew," said Theotokis. "Thank goodness the potion worked!"

The guards loaded every last diamond, and Kakeas let Arxigeas go.

"Take him, take your Arxigeas! I won! Ha ha! I told you, Arxigeas, from now on we will be the greatest enemies. And I'm sure we will meet again!"

And whoosh! He blasted off at full speed.

"Boys, where on earth did you find all those diamonds?" asked Arxigeas.

But everyone was laughing.

"What are you laughing at? We're ruined! We gave Kakeas all the diamonds! Oh, oh, oh, oh!"

"Why are you laughing instead of crying? You should be crying! Aren't you ashamed? I asked you to save me! Oh my goodness, oh my goodness!"

"Arxigeas," said Arxigeena, "it's a trick! A trick!"

"What trick? He took all our diamonds!"

"Theotokis made a magic potion! Come and see how it works!"

Theotokis dropped a grain of sand into the potion, and pop! Out jumped a diamond!

"Oh my goodness, what kind of magic is this?" said Arxigeas. "Are you a wizard?"

"No," said Theotokis. "I just read a book that was lying around here."

"Wait till you see the surprise Kakeas is going to get! He'll put them in his treasure vault, and tomorrow morning when he wakes up, they'll be sand! Boo-hoo-hoo! Boo!"

Meanwhile, Kakeas went into his spaceship, because, remember, he didn't have a planet of his own.

"Perfect," he said. "Tonight I'll sleep hugging my diamonds. I'm rich!"

He lay down and fell asleep.

The next morning he woke up, and what did he see? What did he see, Orfeas?

Sand!

"Sand! Oh my goodness!" He couldn't understand what had happened.

"They robbed me! Guards! Guards! Find whoever stole my diamonds!"

"But nobody came in," said the guards. "You slept all by yourself, and the door was locked!"

Then he realised what had happened. "Oh my goodness, they tricked me! This won't go unpunished! I will get my revenge! I will get my revenge!"

And he kept shouting all by himself. "I will get my revenge! I will get my revenge! And I'm painting the curtains!"

Meanwhile, back on Aphrodite, they were having a party. Not only was Arxigeas back, they had saved their diamonds too!

"Thank you, Theotokis," said Arxigeas. "I don't know how to repay you."

"Are you kidding? That's what friends are for! Don't you worry, don't you worry! All is good, all is good, all is good!"

And they all lived happily ever after... and we lived even better!
