The Costume Festival

Once upon a time, Theotokis and Aristotelis heard that a huge festival was coming to town. Everyone could dress up as whatever they wanted! There would be music, parties, wrestling, ninjas, and who knows what else.

"Oh my goodness," said Theotokis. "I have an idea! Let's invite all our friends!"

"Great idea!" said Aristotelis, and he grabbed the phone. "Hello, Rillas? Do you want to come to a festival? There will be music, wrestling, ninjas..."

"Will there be bananas?" asked Rillas.

"Not the bananas again! Fine, I'll get you bananas too!"

Rilaki heard the news and jumped for joy. "Yes! Let's go!"

"We're going to have the best time!" cheered Dakis.

"I'm so curious to see what's there!" said Akis.

"Taxi! Taxi!" they all shouted, and off they went.

Theotokis had one more idea. "Everybody get dressed up! Put on your costumes and masks, and they'll be amazed!"

But the gorillas and the monkeys just came exactly as they were.

At the gate, the man at the door nearly fell over. "Oh my goodness!" he said. "The most amazing monkey and gorilla costumes I have ever seen in my life!"

"Where are the bananas?" asked Rillas.

"Don't worry, we have lots and lots of bananas!"

Inside, there were people in wild costumes everywhere, and photos being taken all around. Ninjas appeared and vanished in puffs of smoke. Some people were even dressed as famous heroes and villains from games and films!

There was a wrestling ring, and Rilaki cheered so loudly that his fists shot up in the air. "Yes! Yes! Yes!"

Akis found a fighting video game. "This game is awesome!" he said, and everyone wanted a turn.

Then somebody spotted the costume contest. "Come on, you two!" And before they knew it, Rillas and Dakis were up on the big stage.

Rillas flexed his muscles. Dakis struck his funniest pose.

The judges' mouths fell open. "We have never seen such realistic costumes! How did you make them?"

"Ooo-ooo! Ooo-ooo!" said Rillas and Dakis, just like real animals.

"They didn't even make costumes!" gasped the judges. "They've really got into character!"

"Ladies and gentlemen," said the announcer, "we have unanimous winners! We don't need to see anyone else!"

Rillas and Dakis had won a big prize and lots of money!

"Wow!" laughed Rillas. "Now we can buy bananas for a whole year!"

"And I'll carry them all," laughed Theotokis.

As the evening went on, they heard some music. A band was playing guitars and drums, and the songs were really cool.

"We make music too!" said Rilaki proudly. "With our little toy xylophone!"

Everybody danced. Aristotelis and Theotokis danced the most.

Above them, drones lit up the sky, and fireworks burst in every colour. They ate their bananas, watched the ninjas disappear, and laughed and laughed.

Time passed, and at last Theotokis yawned. "I think we're tired now."

"Okay," said Aristotelis, yawning too.

Everybody had a wonderful time.

The End.
