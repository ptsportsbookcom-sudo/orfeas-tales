Orfeas Tales — Story 3
Rillas the Gorilla


Theodokis and Aristotelis found out that ACW wrestling was coming to Nicosia!

"I want to go!" said Aristotelis.

"No way," said Theodokis. "I like books. Let's do something else."

"Come on, you're going to love it! They don't actually hit each other — it's a show! A great show! Please, do me the favour."

Fine, he was convinced.

"Should we bring Rillas too?" said Aristotelis.

"Man, how are we going to bring Rillas? He's a gorilla! How are we going to get him into a wrestling match?"

"Leave it to me, buddy. Let's go ask him."

They hopped on their quad bikes and rode all the way through the jungle. They arrived at Rillas's house and shouted up: "Rillas! Rillas, come downstairs!"

Rillas was half asleep. "What's going on? What do you want?"

"Do you want to go to wrestling on Sunday?"

"But... will there be people there?"

"Yes!"

"And... can I come too?"

"Come on, buddy, don't worry. We'll sort it out. We'll come pick you up next Sunday, alright?"

When Sunday arrived, Aristotelis said: "Come on, Rillas, put these clothes on. You're going to dress up as a wrestler."

"Alright. But guys — will there be bananas? Otherwise I'm not coming."

"Yes, yes, lots of bananas! Don't worry."

"Alright then!"

They rode their quad bikes to the ACW arena. At the entrance, Aristotelis showed his ticket — in he went. Theodokis showed his ticket — in he went. Rillas went to walk in.

"Hold it!" said the doorman. "Only wrestlers in boots are allowed in here."

Rillas looked at him. "Does Nicosia need a wrestler? I am a wrestler! I don't need boots."

"And... will there be bananas?" Rillas asked.

"The winner gets whatever he wants!"

"Oh," said Rillas. "Good idea. Alright then!"

Inside the arena, Aristotelis and Theodokis sat down and waited.

"Where is Rillas? What if they figure out he's a gorilla?"

"I don't know... Come on, sit down and let's watch the show."

The show began. The fighters wrestled, the crowd cheered. Then the announcer said:

"Ladies and gentlemen! Today we have a brand new wrestler. He is... Rillas the Gorilla!"

Theodokis and Aristotelis looked at each other. "Did you hear that? There must be another wrestler called Rillas! What a coincidence!"

"Ladies and gentlemen! In the right corner, Polydefkis the Fox! In the left corner, Rillas the Gorilla! Ding-ding-ding!"

Not even two minutes passed — Rillas grabbed Polydefkis and launched him clean out of the ring. Done!

Then the other athletes came one by one. Not one of them lasted. It didn't even take 30 seconds — bam-boom-bam-boom — he finished them all.

"Oh, Holy Mary!" cried Theodokis. "They're going to realise he's a gorilla, we're going to get in trouble, we'll go to jail!"

"Quiet!" said Aristotelis. "Look at him! The crowd is going absolutely crazy! Rillas! Rillas! Rillas!"

Then came the big announcement:

"Ladies and gentlemen! Because Rillas the Gorilla is so impressive, we have decided on a special challenge: ALL athletes will enter the ring at once, and the last one standing wins whatever he wants!"

Thirty athletes entered. They had made a plan among themselves: "We'll all go after him at the same time! Two by two, three by three. We'll beat him!"

They started. Rillas tossed them left and right like toys. He did one enormous spin — and they flew! At one point there were ten of them on top of him at once.

"Now we've got him!"

GRRRRRR!

He threw all ten of them off! Three remained — he knocked them out too. The crowd exploded!

"Rillas! Rillas! Rillas!"

"Ladies and gentlemen, the winner: Rillas the Gorilla! Mr Rillas, would you like to remove your mask so the crowd can see you?"

"No. I prefer to remain a man of mystery."

"And what prize do you want?"

"I want... a thousand bananas."

"A thousand bananas?"

"Make that a thousand and one!"

"Ladies and gentlemen, we will give him a thousand and one bananas! They will be waiting for him backstage!"

Theodokis found Rillas afterwards. "What happened out there?"

"I walked in through that door, and they all came at me wanting to fight. I decided to protect myself. I was very careful — I didn't cause any damage."

"We were incredibly lucky that nobody figured out what you are!"

"Guys, we have a problem now. How are we going to take a thousand and one bananas home on the quad bikes?"

"We'll sort it out."

They piled everything onto the quad bikes — vroom-vroom-vroom — and rode home. Rillas was very happy. A thousand and one bananas! For a whole week he wouldn't need to do anything. He was going to stay home and rest.

"Hey guys, when are we going to wrestling again?"

Theodokis looked at him. "Never! Because we were lucky we didn't end up in jail."

And they all lived happily ever after, kids!
