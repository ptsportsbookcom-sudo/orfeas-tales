Lost in Limassol

Once upon a time, there was Rillas the gorilla.

One day he said to Rilena, "Listen, I'm going to visit my friends Theotokis and Aristotelis. It's been ages!"

"Do you even know the way?" asked Rilena. "Don't get stressed. Take a phone with you, okay?"

"Okay."

"When are you leaving?"

"On Sunday."

"Alright, alright."

Sunday came. Rillas packed lots of bananas, because it was a long journey, and off he went.

But on the way, a huge storm broke out. Rain poured down and thunder boomed. BOOM! CRASH!

"I can't go on like this," said Rillas. "I need to rest."

He went looking for a cave to shelter in, and without even realising it, he got completely lost.

The next morning he set off again, sure he was going the right way. "I know where I'm going! Excellent, excellent!"

He walked and walked, until at last he saw a city. "Hooray, I made it! Now to find Aristotelis and Theotokis!"

He crept into the city, pressed close to the buildings so that nobody would see him. Then he stopped. "Something strange is going on here... I can see the sea! But the city where Aristotelis and Theotokis live doesn't have a sea! Don't tell me I'm lost?"

He wandered here and there, and the hours went by.

Back home, Rilena tried to call him, but there was no answer. His phone battery had died. He had forgotten to charge it!

So Rilena called Theotokis. "Hello, Theotokis? Has Rillas arrived?"

"No," said Theotokis. "Rillas hasn't come. Why?"

"He should have reached you hours ago! He left yesterday!"

"Oh my goodness, he must be lost! Did he tell you where he was going?"

"He just said he was coming to find you."

"Oh my goodness..." said Theotokis.

Meanwhile, Rillas was in a total panic. The city was so scary: tall buildings everywhere, cars everywhere, horns honking! BEEP! BEEP!

He found a hiding place and squeezed in. "Oh my goodness! How will I get out of here? If people find out there's a gorilla loose, I'll be on the news! Everyone will see me! I have to be very, very careful. I'll wait here a little and think."

Theotokis hung up the phone and ran to Aristotelis. "Hey! Come here, quick! Rillas is lost!"

"Oh my goodness, again?"

"Rillas!"

"Rillas! Again? Again?! Where is he?"

"How should I know? Didn't you give him a phone?"

"I did."

"Then can't you find his phone's signal?"

"Good idea!" said Theotokis.

He opened his laptop and started pressing buttons and typing codes.

"I'm not very good with computers," said Aristotelis. "All this internet stuff..."

At last, Theotokis found the phone's last signal. "Oh my goodness, he's in Limassol! Instead of coming to Nicosia, he went to Limassol! How will we ever find him there?"

"Theotokis, come on!"

"Aristotelis, let's go!"

They jumped on their quads. Diri-diri-di-di-di!

When they reached Limassol, it was chaos! Cars everywhere, police everywhere...

"Luckily there's nothing on the news," said Theotokis. "That means our friend is hiding well!"

"If I were Rillas and I got lost," said Aristotelis, "where would I hide? Where would you hide? Where would you hide? Where would YOU hide?"

"Hey! Stop being silly!"

"Shhh!" said Theotokis. "Let's concentrate. Would he go to the sea? He doesn't like the sea. To the mountains? Maybe. But how, if he's lost?"

"I know!" said Aristotelis. "He'd go to the tallest building! That's what I would do!"

"So where do we look now?"

"Let's find the tallest building in Limassol!"

They found it, but it was full of security guards and cameras.

"Oh my goodness," said Aristotelis. "He's in there for sure, but how do we get in?"

They tried calling him, but his phone was still off.

"If we start shouting, they'll think we're crazy. And to go inside you need permission and ID cards. What do we do? What do we do?"

They thought and thought, until Aristotelis shouted, "I've got it!"

"What have you got?"

"Let's dress up as electricians and say someone called us about a problem!"

"What a great idea!" said Theotokis. "Well done!"

So they dressed up as electricians and walked in.

"Stop, stop, stop!" said the security guard. "Where are you going?"

"Someone called us. There's a problem with the electricity!"

"Which office?"

Aristotelis thought, "What do I say now? I'll just guess..." "The twenty-fifth floor!"

"Ah, that's Mr. Evripides! He's always complaining! Go on up, go on up."

They tiptoed into the lift and went all the way up to the roof.

"Rillas! Rillas!"

Rillas was very frightened. "Am I imagining things, or did I hear my name?"

"Rillas! Are you here?"

"Someone is calling me! Oh no, have the police come?"

"Rillas!"

He peeked out very carefully and saw them. "Oh my goodness!" He jumped out and hugged Theotokis and Aristotelis. "My friends! I came to find you and I got lost! I didn't want to cause a panic like last time, so I stayed up here all night! And now I don't know what to do!"

"It's okay," they said. "We're here now to help you."

"But how will I get down from here?"

"Good question," said Theotokis.

They thought and thought, until they heard a sound. Bzzz... bzzz...

They looked down and saw a man cleaning the windows on a hanging platform.

"Listen, we'll go down and ask that man to bring you down!"

"Okay, okay."

They ran down the stairs as fast as they could. Puff... puff...

"Excuse me, sir, can you bring our friend down?"

"Oh no," said the man. "What happened? Did he do something wrong?"

"No, no! He's just scared of lifts and stairs... and he weighs two hundred kilos!"

"Okay, okay!"

The platform went up, and slowly, slowly, they brought Rillas down. They sat him on a quad and rode him home. Diri-diri-di-di-di!

"Have a bath and sleep here tonight," they told him. "Tomorrow we'll take you back to the jungle. You're not made for stairs!"

Then they called Rilena. "Don't worry, we found him! He was very scared, but tomorrow we'll bring him back to the jungle."

And they all lived happily ever after!

The End.
