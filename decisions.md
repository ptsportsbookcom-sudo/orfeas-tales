# Decision Log

---

**Decision: Arxigeena is the official Story 21 character name**
Date: 2026-09-02
Status: Decided
Topic: Story 21 — Planet 650

**What was decided**
The new planetary guard introduced in Story 21 will officially be named **Arxigeena** in English. The existing character slot `id:11` will be used and unlocked for her debut.

**Why**
• Pantelis explicitly confirmed “Arxigeena” as her name.
• It matches the established English naming pattern used by Arxigeas.
• Reusing `id:11` preserves the existing family-character scaffolding.

**What was considered and rejected**
| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Archigena | Earlier working transliteration; it does not match Pantelis’s confirmed spelling. |
| Add a new character ID | Would leave the intentionally reserved `id:11` slot orphaned. |

**Who was involved**
- Decision maker: Pantelis
- Consulted: Codex
- Informed: Future Orfeas Tales story sessions

**Revisit if**
Only if Pantelis explicitly renames the character in a future story.
