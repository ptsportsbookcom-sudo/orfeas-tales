Hide and Seek in the Jungle

One day, Rilaki found his friends Dakis and Akis. "Guys, I have an awesome idea!" he told them. "What idea? Do you want to play hide-and-seek?" "Yes, yes!" they both shouted.

"Who's going to close their eyes first, I mean who's going to count?" asked Dakis. "I don't mind," said Rilaki, "whoever wants can count." They played a quick counting game to decide, and it landed on Dakis to close his eyes first.

"I've got the perfect hiding spot," Rilaki bragged, "you'll never find me!" Dakis closed his eyes and started counting: "5, 10, 15... 100! Ready or not, here I come!"

Dakis found Akis right away, hiding behind a tree. "That was too easy!" he laughed. "Come on, let's go find Rilaki too."

They searched everywhere, but Rilaki had climbed all the way up the tallest tree in the jungle — nobody could see him up there. And up in that tree, Rilaki fell fast asleep! Dakis and Akis searched for hours, shouting "Rilaki! Rilaki, where are you?" — but Rilaki was sound asleep and wouldn't wake up for anything. Slowly it got dark, and they really started to worry.

"Let's go tell dad we lost Rilaki!" said Dakis. They ran to Maimudakis. "Dad, dad! We were playing hide-and-seek and... we've been looking for hours and can't find Rilaki!"

"Oh no! Run, go find Rillas and Rilena!" Maimudakis cried, worried.

"Rillas! Come down!" "What's going on?" "Have you seen Rilaki?" "No, isn't he playing with Dakis and Akis?" "Oh no, he's lost — we were playing hide-and-seek and we can't find him anywhere!" Everyone started searching together, but it kept getting darker.

"Let's call Theotokis and Aristotelis, ask them to come with the quad bikes and some flashlights," suggested Rillas. The phone rang. "We lost Rilaki! Can you come with your flashlights and the quad bikes?" "Of course! We're on our way!"

Theotokis and Aristotelis arrived quickly on their quad bikes, loaded with flashlights and lanterns. They split into groups — some going right, some left — shining their lights up into the trees and calling out, "Rilaki! Rilaki!"

At some point Rilaki heard the voices and woke up. "Oh no, it's dark! Where is everyone?" He was scared, seeing how high up he was. "Help! Help!" he shouted.

"Shh," said Theotokis, "I hear something, far away!" "Help!" came the cry again. "That's Rilaki! Everyone, over here!"

They reached the tallest tree in the jungle. "Can you believe he climbed all the way up there?" laughed Rillas, and climbed up right away. High up, he found Rilaki crying. "I'm sorry, dad, I didn't know..." "How did you even get up this high? Even I would have a hard time!" "I guess I'm just really lucky," Rilaki said, laughing through his tears, "I got really scared up there!"

They all climbed back down. "I'm sorry, everyone, I didn't mean to — I just fell asleep," said Rilaki. "It's okay, it's okay. But next time, Rilaki, be careful not to fall asleep while you're hiding! If they can't find you within 5 to 10 minutes, you win — deal?" "Deal!" said Dakis and Akis too.

They all went home happy, because Rilaki was safe and sound. And they lived happily ever after!
