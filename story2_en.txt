Rillas the Gorilla

Once upon a time, there were Theotokis and Aristotelis.

Theotokis called Aristotelis on the phone and said: "Hey, do you want to go to the zoo?"

"What would I do at the zoo?"

"Come on! There are animals there — it'll be great!"

"Alright, alright."

They jumped on their four-wheel dirt bikes — VROOM VROOM VROOM! — and zoomed off to the zoo.

They looked at the lions, the turtles, the snakes. They walked around and saw everything.

Then, they passed a cage with a single gorilla sitting alone. All by himself.

As they walked past, they heard: "Psssst! Hey! Over here!"

"Did you hear something?"

"I heard... something. I don't know..."

"Hey — turn around!"

They turned around and stared at the gorilla. The gorilla was staring right back at them.

"Wait — are YOU talking to us?!"

"Yes, I'm talking to you! Did you not know I can talk? I've been listening to humans for years — I learned. My name is Rillas the Gorilla!"

The two boys stood there with their mouths wide open.

"I'm Theotokis, and this is my best friend Aristotelis. How can we help you, Rillas?"

"Boys, I've been trapped in here for so long. I'm not happy. I want to go to the jungle. Can you help me?"

"We don't know... do you have a plan?"

"I chose you two because you seem like good kids. Will you be my friends?"

Theotokis and Aristotelis stepped aside and whispered to each other.

"We should help him! Let's help him!"

They turned back. "Okay Rillas — do you have a plan?"

"Yes! Listen carefully. Every Friday night, there's a guard who is very bad at his job — he falls asleep all night long! If you can sneak over and grab the keys, you can open my cage. But the cameras — I'll cause a panic with the other gorillas as a distraction, and while everyone is running around, you grab the keys and let me out."

"Yes, yes! But not this Friday — next week. We'll come back first to watch the zoo and map everything out, then we'll tell you we're ready."

"Perfect."

So Theotokis and Aristotelis made their plan. They had to find the sleepy guard, and find a path where the cameras couldn't see them.

The following Friday they spotted the guard — just sitting there, looking left, looking right, barely staying awake.

On Saturday they found all the camera blind spots and mapped a route no camera could catch.

"Alright — and we wear our caps to hide our faces!"

The week passed. Friday arrived. Just before the zoo closed, they saw the guard was already nodding off. They had one hour to pull this off. Caps on, they slipped inside.

The guard was slumped on his bench, fast asleep.

They crept silently along their mapped route and grabbed the keys from the guard's belt.

"Okay Rillas — NOW! Cause the panic!"

"Got it!"

Rillas ran over to the other gorillas and said something to them — and it started. CHAOS! Gorillas everywhere! The guards were running around, throwing bananas trying to calm the gorillas down.

Theotokis and Aristotelis sprinted to Rillas's cage and unlocked it.

"Quick, quick! RUN! Get out before they catch you!"

"But where do I go?!"

"OUT of the zoo — climb a tree!"

Rillas burst out of the cage and scrambled up a tree just outside the zoo walls. The boys ran out after him — and that's when they realised something was very wrong.

The whole street was blocked. Police cars everywhere.

Someone had already noticed a gorilla was missing.

Then they heard it — a helicopter overhead.

"Oh no," whispered Theotokis. "Are we going to jail?!"

"Relax — we wait until the police leave."

They crept under Rillas's tree and whispered up at him.

"Rillas! Stay up there — stay still! We'll come back for you when it's safe!"

"Okay, okay."

They waited. And waited. The whole night passed.

Finally, as dawn broke, the police packed up and left. They said they'd come back in the morning.

Now was their chance.

They took Rillas to his new home — a tall tree deep in a quiet, hidden spot where no car could ever reach. It was perfect.

They sat together and ate bananas.

And from that day on, the three of them became the best of friends. They went on many adventures together — and every one of them is another story.

The End.
