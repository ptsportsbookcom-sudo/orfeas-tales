# Story 21 — Final Asset Mapping

Story 21 uses all 37 selected images. The original descriptive PNG files remain in `story21/panels/`; the website-ready sequence is `s21p01.webp` through `s21p37.webp`.

| Panels | Narrative beat |
|---|---|
| 01–03 | Phone call, quad-bike journey, Rillas receives the invitation |
| 04–07 | Rilaki and the monkeys prepare and board the spaceship |
| 08–11 | Cockpit, blast-off, navigation, and the wrong-planet landing |
| 12–17 | Arxigeena’s arrival, confrontation, introductions, and deal |
| 18–23 | Planet 650 chores: rocks, cleaning, and glowing fruit |
| 24–27 | Departure for Aphrodite, radio call, and Arxigeas waking |
| 28–31 | Arrival, Arxigeena’s introduction, and Arxigeas’s reaction |
| 32–33 | Celebration beneath Aphrodite’s moons |
| 34–35 | Autopilot setup and Arxigeas offering Arxigeena a ride home |
| 36–37 | Goodbye through the window and the final flight to Planet 650 |
