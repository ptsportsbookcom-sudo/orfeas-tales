Rillas Goes to Africa!

Once upon a time, there were Theotokis and Aristotelis.

One day, Aristotelis said to Theotokis, "Hey, do you think Rillas has ever been to Africa?"

"Hmm, I don't think so. Why do you ask?"

"Well, he's a gorilla! How can a gorilla never go to Africa to see what's happening there?"

"Good point. But how will he get there? He has to travel. He needs tickets."

"We'll sort it out for him!"

"Oh yeah?"

"Yeah! Remember when we took him to the wrestling?"

"And remember when we took him to the beach, and the police almost caught us?"

"It's fine, we'll sort it out. Don't worry!"

"Okay, let's call him and ask him."

They called Rillas on the phone. "Hey, Rillas! What are you up to?"

"Hey, boys! I'm right here, chilling. Today I decided I'm not doing anything at all. Just relaxing, nice and slow."

"Aristotelis wants to know if you'd like to go to Africa."

"Go where?"

"To Africa!"

"Why would I go to Africa?"

"There are lots of gorillas there! You can go and meet your relatives."

"Boys... are there bananas?"

"Of course there are bananas! Gorillas live there, so they eat bananas, so there are bananas!"

"And how are we going to get there?"

"We'll get on an airplane."

"A what?"

"An airplane. Imagine a big bird that flies..."

"Oh no! I'm not getting inside a flying bird! What if it falls?"

"It's safe, Rillas, it's safe! Trust us!"

"Hmm... can't I just ride on the roof of the airplane?"

"No way! You'd fall off!"

"Oh, all right," said Rillas. "But only I will come, just in case. Rilena and Rilaki will stay here."

"Okay, okay!"

Aristotelis and Theotokis went inside to look for tickets. They searched here and there, and they found cheap tickets for three.

But now they had a new problem. How would they dress up a gorilla so he could get on a plane?

They booked the tickets and called Rillas. "We'll come and get you on the day of the trip!"

"Okay, okay!"

The big day came. The boys jumped on their quad bikes, VROOM VROOM VROOM, and went to get Rillas.

"You have to wear these clothes," they told him. They had brought him a hat, a long coat, sunglasses and trousers.

"What's this?" said Rillas. "Trousers?"

"Just put them on and don't say a word!"

"And shoes too?! I think you two have gone completely bananas!"

"Rillas, do you want to go to Africa or not?"

"I want to go to Africa."

"Then put on the shoes!"

At the airport, Theotokis started to panic. "Oh my goodness! We have to go through security! They'll find out he's a gorilla, and we'll go to jail!"

"Leave it to me," said Aristotelis.

And there was another problem, Orfeas: Rillas had no passport!

Theotokis took Rillas by the hand and walked up to the police officer.

"Whoa, whoa, whoa!" said the officer. "Where do you think you're going without a passport?"

"Oh, Officer, I'm sorry," said Theotokis. "This is my dad. He always wanted to go to Africa, but he lost his passport, and we paid so much money for the tickets..."

"That's not my problem," said the officer. "No passport, no ID, no flight! No, no, no!"

"What do we do now?" asked Aristotelis.

"Leave it to me," said Theotokis.

They took a photo of Rillas in his hat and sunglasses. Theotokis had an old pretend ID card from one of their games, and he stuck the photo right on it.

They went back to the officer. He looked at the card. He looked at it again. And again.

Theotokis was sweating buckets. "He knows it's fake! We're going to jail!"

"Okay, go ahead," said the officer.

"Phew!" said the boys. "What a relief!"

On the plane, they whispered, "Rillas, whatever you do, keep your clothes on, or everyone on this plane will go crazy!"

"Okay, okay!"

The flight was many hours long, Orfeas. Luckily, Rillas fell fast asleep, and nobody noticed him.

Then a voice came on: "Announcement, announcement! This is your pilot, Pikos Apikos. We will land in ten minutes. Please fasten your seatbelts for a nice, smooth landing."

Rillas woke up. "Oh no! I need the toilet! I need the toilet right now!"

He ran to the toilet. But he forgot to lock the door, and he took off his hat!

Just then, a man opened the door. "AAAAH!" he screamed. "A GORILLA!"

He ran down the aisle. "Help! Help! There's a gorilla in the toilet! Call the firefighters! Call somebody!"

Meanwhile, Rillas quickly put his hat back on and tiptoed back to his seat.

"Calm down, sir," said the flight attendant. "Are you sure you weren't dreaming?"

"Go and look! Right now!"

The flight attendant opened the toilet door. There was nobody inside, Orfeas!

"Oh my goodness," whispered Aristotelis. "That was close!"

The plane landed, and they got through the checks. Then they found a guide to take them on a safari into the jungle.

They all climbed into a big jeep and drove out of the city. Wow, Orfeas! They saw an oasis, giant mountains and giant trees!

Rillas got more and more excited. The wild gorilla inside him was waking up!

Suddenly he was gone! He threw off his disguise and climbed up into the trees, doing flips and somersaults like a real wild gorilla.

"Oh no!" said Theotokis. "The gorilla inside him has woken up! We're going to lose him! Follow him!"

"Follow who?" said the guide. "I thought he was a man!"

"Don't worry, he's friendly. We'll pay you a little extra."

"Okay, okay!" The guide chased him in the jeep, while Rillas jumped from tree to tree. "OOOH! OOOH!"

After a while, Rillas found other gorillas. "Oh my goodness! This place is full of gorillas!"

He talked to them in human words, but they didn't understand. "Of course! I have to speak their language!" So he spoke gorilla.

The gorillas told him they were a family who lived far away from people. Every day they had a wonderful time.

"Are there bananas?" asked Rillas.

"Of course there are bananas! Have some!"

They told him that in the jungle they were free. Nobody bothered them, and they woke up whenever they wanted.

"Just like me!" said Rillas. "But my jungle is a bit smaller."

"Here it's huge!"

"And don't you have any problems here?"

"Sometimes. Lions come by, and leopards..."

"You see? It's the same everywhere," said Rillas. "But you're stronger here. Well done! And your bananas are amazing! The best bananas I've ever eaten in my whole life!"

Then Rillas went to the boys. "Boys, listen. I'm having a wonderful time. I want to stay and get to know my relatives. You go to the city, and come back in a week so we can meet up."

"Okay, okay!"

So Theotokis and Aristotelis went to a big city in Nigeria. They walked around, ate yummy food, rested and had an awesome time, Orfeas.

When the week was over, they asked the guide, "Please take us back to the jungle."

"Okay!" he said, and took them back.

But where was the gorilla? "Rillas! Rillas!" They couldn't find him anywhere, Orfeas. And all the gorillas looked the same!

"Oh no! The plane leaves tomorrow! We lost our friend! What will we tell Rilena?"

Suddenly they saw a gorilla racing through the trees, doing flips.

"Hey, what if that's Rillas? Look how strong he's got! He's like a beast!"

It was Rillas! "Hey, Rillas, what's going on?"

"Oh, boys, you won't believe it! I had the best time! My relatives are awesome! I think I'm staying here!"

"What are you talking about?" said Theotokis.

"I'm staying! Don't worry about me!"

But suddenly, Orfeas, they heard loud noises: BANG! BOOM! BANG! BOOM!

"Oh my goodness, what's that?" said Rillas.

Theotokis took out his binoculars and looked this way and that way. "Hunters! Hunters are coming!"

"Hunters? What are hunters?"

"They hunt anything that moves! Gorillas, monkeys, anything!"

"Brothers!" Rillas said to the gorillas. "You didn't tell me about this!"

"Oh, it's fine, they only come once in a while..."

"No, no, no!" said Rillas. "I'm going home right now!"

He put his disguise back on, they jumped into the jeep, and off they went, with BANG! BOOM! BANG! BOOM! behind them.

"We're going home," said Theotokis. "It's way too dangerous here!"

"That's not what they told me!" said Rillas. "Seven days, and everything was perfect! We ate, we played... and now this? No, no, no!"

They got on the plane and flew all the way back home to their own jungle.

"Did you have a good time?" Rilena asked.

"I had a great time, my Rilena. But there's no place like home! I love you and Rilaki so much. I don't want to be anywhere else!"

And they lived happily ever after, and we lived even better.
