The Twins Are Coming!

Once upon a time, there was our friend Arxigeas. One day, he found out some big news: Arxigeena was going to have a baby!

"Oh my goodness!" he said. "I have to call my friends and tell them!"

He called Theotokis on the phone. Ring ring... ring ring...

"Hey, Theotokis!"

"What's up, my friend?"

"We're pregnant! We're..."

"You're WHAT?"

"We're pregnant! Arxigeena is going to have a baby!"

"Wow! Congratulations!"

"Tell all our friends. When the babies are born, you all have to come over for a big party!"

"The babies?" asked Theotokis.

"Yes, yes! We're having twins! Two little boys!"

"Oh my goodness! And when are they coming?"

"Well, we aliens are fast. We don't wait nine months like you do on Earth. In three months, my babies will be ready!"

"Three months?!"

"Yes, yes, yes!"

"Okay! Then we'll wait. Call us a little before they're born, so we know what's happening."

"Okay, okay!"

One month went by. Then two months.

"Oh my goodness," Arxigeas kept saying. "My babies are coming! Am I ready? Am I not ready?"

"Relax," said Arxigeena. "Everything will be fine. At first it's hard with little babies, but after that it's wonderful."

"But am I ready?" said Arxigeas. "Will they love me? Won't they love me? I don't know what's going to happen! Help! I'm so nervous!" And he cried a little, because he was so worried.

You know, Orfeas, aliens have their babies after only three months. And so the big day came closer and closer.

"Come on," Arxigeena told him. "Pack the suitcase for the hospital, and get yourself ready too. The babies could come any day now!"

"What do you mean, any day now? I'm not ready!"

"Shh! Focus, please!" said Arxigeena. "What do you mean, you're not ready? The babies won't wait for you! They're coming!"

"Oh my goodness... okay."

Arxigeas and Arxigeena lived on Planet 650 now. Their plan was simple: when it was time, they would fly to Aphrodite, Arxigeas's planet, and have the babies there, because everything was easier there. The spaceship was ready.

One day, Arxigeas had to fly from Planet 650 to Aphrodite to run some errands.

"Keep your phone with you," Arxigeena told him. "The babies could come at any moment!"

"Okay," he said. "I'll keep it with me."

He took his spaceship, flew to Aphrodite and went off to do his errands.

Suddenly, his phone rang!

"Arxigeas, where are you? Come right now! The babies are coming!"

"Okay! I'm getting into the spaceship right now, and I'm on my way!"

He ran to where he had parked his spaceship... and the spaceship was gone!

"Wait," he said. "Is it because I'm so nervous? Maybe I didn't park here?"

He looked this way. Nothing. He looked that way. Nothing. He kept pressing the button on his key and asking everyone, "Have you seen my spaceship?" Nobody had.

Then he understood. Someone had stolen his spaceship!

"Oh my goodness! What am I going to do? Arxigeena is having the babies, and I can't get to Planet 650! I'm going straight to the police!"

He ran to the police station.

"Please! Someone stole my spaceship! Somebody has to take me to Planet 650!"

"Impossible right now," said the police. "There's a big fire on Aphrodite. All the police officers and all the fire engines have gone to help. And nobody is allowed to fly away from Aphrodite today."

"Oh my goodness, what am I going to do?" said Arxigeas. "I have to find my spaceship! Wait a minute... if nobody can fly away from Aphrodite, that means the thieves are still here! I'm going to find them!"

He thought and thought. "Let me go back to the place where I parked."

There was a shop there, Orfeas, and it had a security camera.

"Excuse me," he said to the shop owner. "My wife is having our babies, and someone stole my spaceship! Please, can I look at your camera?"

"Of course! What time?"

"Around ten o'clock."

They watched the video, and there they were: two aliens from Aphrodite, climbing into Arxigeas's spaceship and flying away in one direction.

"Thank you so much!" said Arxigeas.

He went that way, to another shop a little further down. "Please, my wife is having our babies! Can I see your camera too?" Camera after camera, he looked at five or six of them, and then he figured it out.

"They're going to the spaceship junkyard! They want to take my spaceship apart and sell the pieces! I have to run!"

He reached the junkyard and sneaked inside like a ninja. He hid here. He hid there. He hid behind old broken spaceships so nobody would see him. And there were the thieves!

"What a beautiful spaceship," said one thief. "We'll take it apart and sell it for lots and lots of diamonds!"

"When do we start?"

"Tonight! Right now! Before the owner comes looking for it and finds us!"

Arxigeas crept closer and closer. Then he jumped out!

He did a somersault and — WHACK! — kicked the first thief. Then he did a spinning kick and — WHACK! — got the second one too. They fell to the ground and didn't know what had happened.

They jumped back up. "Grrr! We'll get you!"

"Boys," said Arxigeas, "nobody can beat me today. My wife is having our babies, and I have to go!"

"So what?" said the thieves. "Why should we care if your wife is having babies?"

"You may not care, but I do!"

He grabbed the first thief by the arm, spun him around in the air and — BAAAM! — threw him a hundred metres away! He grabbed the second thief by the leg, spun him around and — BAAAM! — another hundred metres!

But these thieves were strong too. They got up and came running back. So Arxigeas grabbed them both and — BOING! — bumped their heads together! Down they went, fast asleep, with stars spinning around their heads.

He jumped into his spaceship. "Arxigeena, I'm coming! Have the babies been born?"

"You might make it, or you might not!" she said. "Hurry!"

He flew at full speed to Planet 650, and he arrived just as the babies were coming.

"We don't have time to go to Aphrodite," said Arxigeena. "We're having the babies right here!"

"Oh no!"

"Arxigeas, are you listening? There's no time! Bring me some towels, sit here and hold my hand. The doctors are already here!"

It took a little while, Orfeas, and then... the first baby was born!

He cried and cried. He was a little boy, and Arxigeas was so happy.

"We're not finished yet!" said Arxigeena.

"Oh! I forgot!"

Five, six, seven minutes later, the second baby was born.

Arxigeas held them both in his arms. "Right," he said. "The one who came first will be called Geas. And my second son will be called... Arxigeas! Oops! I mean Eas!"

"So I name you Geas, and you Arxigeas... oops! You're Eas!" He laughed. "I'm so excited that I keep giving you my own name! One more time: you are Geas, and you are Eas, and you are my sons! Thank you, Arxigeena."

"Arxigeena, how are you feeling?"

"I'm very well," she said, "but I'm tired. Let me sleep a little. You look after our babies, and I'll see you tomorrow!"

Then Arxigeas called all his friends. "Guys, the babies are here!"

He sent them photos. "This is Geas, and this is Eas! You'll meet them very soon!"

Rillas saw the photos too, and he jumped for joy!

The new family would make their home on Aphrodite.

And they all lived happily ever after... and we lived even better!
