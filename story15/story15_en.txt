Rilena's Trick

Once upon a time, Rilena and Maimudakena got tired of Rillas and Maimudakis never helping around the house. They slept all day, ate all day, and did absolutely nothing else.

Rilena went to find Maimudakena: "Tell me honestly — does Maimudakis help around your house at all?" "No," she said, "does yours?" "He does nothing! We have to do something about this," said Rilena. "I have an idea. We'll ask Aristotelis and Theotokis to host us for two nights, while the two of them are left alone with the kids." "Deal."

Rilena called Theotokis: "Hey Theotokis, how are you?" "Good, you?" "I need to tell you something. Rillas and Maimudakis never help around the house, and we want to teach them a lesson — a little prank." "What kind of prank?" "Maimudakena and I will come stay at your place for two days." "And what will you tell Rillas and Maimudakis?" "Nothing! We'll just disappear. We'll only tell the kids, so they don't worry." "Alright, alright."

Aristotelis was thrilled when he heard: "Rilena and Maimudakena are coming to visit? Great idea — that'll teach them not to forget the housework!"

Rilena told Rilaki: "I'm going on a two-day vacation to Theotokis and Aristotelis's, but don't tell your dad, okay?" "Okay." Maimudakena told Dakis and Akis the same thing: "We're going on vacation there too, don't tell your dad." "Okay, okay."

That night, once everyone was asleep, Rilena snuck out and met up with Maimudakena. "Let's go." "Do you know the way?" "Yes, Theotokis sent me a map." They arrived, found the house, and settled in for the night.

The next morning, Rillas woke up. "Rilena! What are we eating today?" No answer. He checked the bedroom, Rilaki's room, the bathroom, upstairs, downstairs — nowhere to be found. He woke Rilaki. "Dad, I'm hungry!" "Mom! Mom!" Rillas called out. Still nothing. "How should I know where Mom is?" said Rilaki. "What do you usually eat in the morning?" "She makes me pancakes." "Pancakes? I have no idea how to make those! I'll get you a big bowl of cereal instead!" He poured a huge bowl of cereal, and they waited.

Noon came and went with no sign of Rilena. "Let me go check on my friend Maimudakis, maybe he knows something." He arrived to find the exact same problem. "Are you okay?" "No! My wife disappeared too, just like that!" said Maimudakis. "What a coincidence, I have the exact same problem! The kids woke up early, I can't find their clothes, I don't know what to do!" "Let's get organized. Come stay at our place, all of you." "Alright, alright."

Maimudakis brought Dakis and Akis over to Rillas and Rilaki's house. Night fell and everyone was hungry. "What should we cook?" One knew a dish his mom used to make, the other knew a different one. In the end, "Eat some salad and soup," they told the kids, "and sleep here tonight."

Once the kids were asleep, Maimudakis suddenly remembered: "We have to wash the dishes! Do the laundry! Clean the house! How do they manage all this every single day? I hope they're okay." "Let's hope nothing bad happened to them," said Rillas. "I have no idea where they even went..."

The next morning, Dakis, Akis, and Rilaki were all crying at once — one was hungry, another wanted this, a third wanted that. Rillas and Maimudakis were completely at a loss. "Let's take them to the forest amusement park," they suggested. They went, with swings, a pool, and slides — but made the mistake of letting the kids eat way too much candy.

By the time they got home, the kids had gone wild with energy! They tore up clothes, flung paint everywhere, turned the whole house upside down. Exhausted, Rillas and Maimudakis tried to keep up, but the kids kept shouting "We're hungry! We're hungry!" "I can't take it anymore," said Rillas. "Let's call Aristotelis and Theotokis — maybe they know something."

They called. "Theotokis, what's going on over there?" "I'm okay!" Rillas answered, desperate. "Rilena and Maimudakena disappeared! The kids have driven us crazy, we've done laundry, mopped floors, I can't take it anymore!" "One second," said Theotokis, and put them on speaker. "Rilena, come here!" Rilena picked up. "Have you been at Theotokis's this whole time?" Rillas asked. "How's it going at home?" she asked back, laughing. "Terrible! We're exhausted." "Now you see how hard we work every day? You need to help more. We'll be back the day after tomorrow. And we want the house spotless when we get there — tell Maimudakis too." "Okay," said Rillas, humbled.

Rillas explained everything to Maimudakis: "They're at Theotokis and Aristotelis's — because we never help around the house. We need to step it up."

The next day, Rillas and Maimudakis put the kids to work too, and both houses were scrubbed sparkling clean. The kids fed them, cooked for them, and everyone learned to pull their weight.

On the third day, Rilena knocked on the door. "Rillas, come outside! Inspection time!" She walked in and checked every corner of the house. "Very well done!" Maimudakena did the same at her house — and found everything spotless too. "Are you going to pull this disappearing act on the housework again?" "No, no! We learned our lesson!" they both promised. And they all lived happily ever after — even better than before!
