The Fish of the Jungle

Once upon a time, Theotokis and Aristotelis decided to go fishing.

"Hey, should we take our friend the gorilla with us?" said Aristotelis.

"Yes, but won't they make lots of noise? You know what the gorillas are like..." said Theotokis.

"No, not all of them. Just one. Today we'll take little Rilaki. Let's call him."

"Okay, okay."

They phoned him. "Hey, Rilaki! Come over, let's go somewhere!"

"Where are we going?"

"We're going fishing."

"Fishing? What do I do there? What do I even wear?"

"Fishing is easy. You take a rod, put bait on the hook, cast it into the water and relax. And you catch fish."

"And what am I supposed to do all that time? I'll just eat a banana."

"Here we go again with the bananas!" laughed the boys. "Don't worry, we'll bring you bananas."

"Okay, let's go! I've never tried it before. Let's see what it's like."

"There's just one rule," said Theotokis. "When you're fishing, you have to be quiet. Only quiet."

"Okay, okay."

They got their quad bikes ready and packed fishing rods, bait and lots of food. Brrrm, brrrm, brrrm... and off they went.

"Is there a lake around here with big fish?" asked Aristotelis.

"There is," said Rilaki. "A big lake, deep in the jungle."

"Let's go there!"

They arrived, parked their quad bikes, set everything up on the ground and got the bait ready.

"So what do we do now?" asked Rilaki.

"We put the bait on the hook, to catch the fish."

"And what do you do with the fish?"

"Nothing! We just look at them and let them go. We came to have a nice time."

"Oh. Okay, okay."

Each of them took a rod and cast it into the water. They waited... and waited... nothing.

"But I can't do this all day!" said Rilaki.

"Shhh!" said Theotokis. "Quiet!"

Time passed... and more time passed... nothing.

"Boys, is anything ever going to happen?"

"Shhh! Quiet!"

Then, all of a sudden... FSSST! Something moved in the water.

"What was that?" said Aristotelis.

FSSST, FSSST, FSSST...

"Oh my goodness, that thing looks really big!"

"Didn't you want fish?" said Rilaki. "There are fish in this lake, all right."

"How big are they?"

"Very big. About twenty meters!"

"Twenty meters?! Come on, you're teasing us!"

"You know what? Let's go home," said Theotokis.

But just as they were about to leave... a huge fish grabbed Theotokis's rod and pulled him into the lake!

Theotokis held on to the rod with both hands, and the fish dragged him across the water, zooming back and forth like a jet ski!

"HELP! HELP!"

"What is he doing?" said Aristotelis. "Instead of fishing, he's water-skiing!" And he took out his phone and filmed a video. "Look at Theotokis having so much fun! I'm so jealous!"

"Help! I'm scared I'll fall in! HELP!"

Then Rilaki took a good look. "Wait. He's not okay. Look at him, he's terrified! He's calling us!"

"Are you serious?" said Aristotelis.

"Look at him! He's crying!"

"Oh my goodness, you're right. He really is scared!"

The fish went back and forth, deeper and deeper. The lake was huge.

"Help! I can't let go of the rod! I'm scared, I'm scared!"

"Oh my goodness, what do we do now?"

"I have an idea," said Rilaki. "Let's make lots of noise, so the fish comes closer and lets our friend go!"

They shouted, they clapped, they made a huge racket. Nothing. The fish kept going back and forth, because the hook had got stuck.

Suddenly, the fish leaped high out of the water... SPLASH!... and leaped again.

"Oh my goodness!" cried Theotokis. "It's huge! It's a three-meter fish! It's prehistoric!"

"Enough! Save me! We have to do something!"

"What do we do? What do we do?"

"Where's the food?" said Rilaki. "Throw it in the water!"

"Our food? No way, we'll go hungry!" said Aristotelis.

"Your friend is in trouble! Bring the food, before I get angry!"

"Here's the plan," said Rilaki. "When the fish comes for the food, I'll be holding a big log, and I'll bonk it right on the head, so it gets dizzy. And you, Aristotelis, swim out and grab Theotokis."

"Okay, okay."

They threw in half of the food. Here comes the fish... VZZZOOOM! Rilaki swung the log... and missed! The fish was much too fast.

"Help! Help!" shouted Theotokis.

"One last try," said Rilaki. "Do we have any food left? Don't worry, you'll be fine."

They threw in the last of the food. One... two... three... BONK!

Rilaki bonked the fish right on the head. The fish got dizzy and stopped. Aristotelis dived in, swam out and grabbed his friend.

They pulled Theotokis out of the water, dripping wet. He was cold and shaking with fright. And the giant fish shook its head and swam slowly back into the deep.

"Thank you, boys. You saved me! I had no idea what was going to happen. These are the fish of the jungle!"

"That's how fish are in the jungle, buddy!" said Rilaki.

"Well, next time..." began Aristotelis.

"There is no next time!" said Theotokis. "Next time we're going to Xyliatos Dam, where the fish are tiny and silly!"

Luckily, Theotokis was safe, and they all went home.

And they lived happily ever after... and we lived even better!
