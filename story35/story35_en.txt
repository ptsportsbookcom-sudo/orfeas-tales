The Law of the Duel

Once upon a time, there was the baddest villain in the whole universe. His name was Kakeas, and he lived in space.

Kakeas didn't have a planet of his own. So he went around stealing other people's planets!

One day, he made up his mind and flew to Planet 650, Arxigeena's planet.

He had an enormous spaceship. He swooped down, his huge army marched out, and they captured everyone on Planet 650. Arxigeena was there too!

But just in time, Arxigeena managed to send a secret message to Arxigeas.

"We have a problem! A villain called Kakeas has come in a huge, scary spaceship, and he has captured all of us!"

"Oh my goodness!" cried Arxigeas. "What am I going to do? What am I going to do?"

He called his friend Aristotelis on the phone. Ring ring... ring ring...

"Hello?"

"Hey! It's me, Arxigeas, my friend!"

"What's going on?"

"We have a big problem! A villain came, Kakeas, the biggest villain in the whole universe, and he captured everyone on Planet 650. And my Arxigeena is there!"

"Oh my goodness! What are we going to do?"

"I don't know. I need help!"

"Okay. I'll get the others and we'll come. We'll jump in the spaceship, but it's a three-day trip."

"Okay, okay!"

Aristotelis called Theotokis right away and told him everything.

"Oh my goodness! We're leaving right now! Let's go and get Rillas and Maimudakis!"

The boys jumped on their quad bikes and raced to the jungle. Brrrm, brrrm, brrrm!

"Friends, Kakeas has kidnapped Arxigeena!"

"Of course we're coming to help our friend Arxigeas!" said Rillas.

They all climbed into the spaceship. "3, 2, 1... blast off!" Whoooosh!

Rillas didn't even remember his bananas. All he cared about was helping their friend.

Three days later, they landed on Aphrodite.

"What are we going to do?" Arxigeas asked Theotokis.

"We have to think of something," said Theotokis. "Don't you have some kind of battle code here in space? Long ago, on Earth, you could tell your enemy that you wanted to duel."

"And he had to accept?"

"Yes! And then you fought with swords!"

"I don't know," said Arxigeas. "Maybe there's something in a book. Come to my library."

Theotokis read a bit here and a bit there, and suddenly he found a book called "The Rules of Duels with Space Villains".

"Oh, this is very interesting!" he said.

He read and read, until he found a paragraph:

"When an alien captures someone else's planet, anyone has the right to challenge him to a duel. The two must fight alone, without their armies. But the villain gets to choose the weapon."

So the plan was ready. Arxigeas would say: "I challenge you to a duel! If I win, you will leave Planet 650 and never come back. If you win, I will be your servant."

"Let's go!" said Arxigeas.

"Yes, but we must be careful," said Theotokis. "They might shoot down our spaceship before we get there."

So they decided to send Kakeas a message just before they arrived.

They climbed into the spaceship. "I wonder which weapon he'll choose," said Arxigeas. "We'll see soon enough," said Theotokis.

They reached Planet 650. Luckily, nobody shot at them. But soldiers were waiting. "Freeze! Freeze!"

"No!" shouted Arxigeas. "I call upon the Law of the Duel!"

"Oh no," said the soldiers. "How does he know about that law? He tricked us! Fine, we'll take you to our boss."

They took him to Kakeas.

"Who are you?" asked Kakeas.

"I am Arxigeas! You have kidnapped my wife, Arxigeena, and I have come to save the honour of Planet 650!"

"And I am Kakeas, the biggest villain in the whole universe! Ha ha ha ha! I captured Planet 650, and I will beat you too!"

"No," said Arxigeas. "I call upon the Law of the Duel. And you know what that means."

"What does it mean?" asked Kakeas. He went to find his advisor. "Psst! What is this Law of the Duel? How come I never heard of it?"

"Me neither, boss. I'll go and read about it, so we don't look silly."

He went and found the books, came back, and said: "Hmm... The Law of the Duel says you must accept the duel. If he wins, you must leave the planet. If you win, you set the terms."

"I accept!" said Kakeas. "So... what was your name again? Tiny-eas?"

"No! Arxigeas!"

"What? Silly-eas?"

"No! Arxigeas!"

"What... Feather-eas?"

"AR-XI-GE-AS!"

"Ah, Arxigeas, okay, okay. Here are my terms. If you win, I'll leave Planet 650. But if I win, you will be my servant... and I will marry Arxigeena! Ha ha ha!"

"Well," said Arxigeas, "there's nothing else we can do. We accept."

"And now," said Kakeas, "since you challenged me, I choose the weapons!"

"Come on," thought Arxigeas. "Please choose swords... please choose swords..."

"And I choose... swords! Because I have a very beautiful sword, and I have never used it!"

"What is he saying?" thought Arxigeas. "Doesn't he know how to use a sword?"

"Ha ha ha! The duel will be tomorrow, at twelve o'clock!"

So they went back to Arxigeas's planet. Arxigeas took his sword and practised with Rillas and Maimudakis.

Meanwhile, Kakeas just sat and stared at his sword. "What a beautiful sword! It's so huge!"

"Do you know how to use it?" asked his advisor.

"No! But look at it! It's so powerful! As soon as he sees it, he'll get such a fright that he'll run away!"

"Excuse me, Mr Kakeas... but shouldn't you practise?"

"Get out of here! Does Kakeas practise? Out!"

Arxigeas, on the other hand, was so worried that he couldn't sleep. "If he chose swords, he must be very, very good. Oh no, we're going to lose... and he's going to marry Arxigeena!"

The next day came, and everyone gathered. Kakeas even brought Arxigeena along to watch.

The rule was simple: whoever can't keep fighting loses.

Arxigeas arrived with his sword. It was a nice sword... but quite small.

Then Kakeas arrived. He was a giant, and his sword weighed a hundred kilos! But he held it as if it were a feather. "Ha ha ha! Now you'll see how you lose!"

"Oh dear," said Arxigeas. "We're done for."

"Oh my goodness," said Theotokis. "Look at that enormous sword! He's holding it like a kitchen knife! We've lost!"

"Are you ready?" asked the advisor. "Yes! 3, 2, 1... begin!"

Kakeas began. Swish, swoosh... he swung the giant sword this way and that... and TSIOU! The sword flew right out of his hands!

"What just happened?!"

Arxigeas charged at once and pointed his sword at Kakeas. "Do you surrender?"

"No, I don't surrender!"

BONK! Arxigeas gave him one tap on the head with the flat of his sword. It didn't hurt him, but down went Kakeas, flat on the ground!

"Do you surrender now?"

"I surrender," said Kakeas. "I made a mistake. I should have chosen laser blasters... but my sword was so beautiful!"

"Wait a minute," said Arxigeas. "Have you ever even held a sword before?"

"No. But it was so beautiful, I thought I'd win just because it was beautiful!"

Everyone burst out laughing.

"Rules are rules," said Kakeas. "I will leave. But remember this, Arxigeas: from now on, we are the biggest enemies in the universe. I will never forget what happened today. We'll meet again... and one day I'll come and conquer Aphrodite too! Mwahahahaha!"

And he took his army and flew away.

Everyone on Planet 650 cheered. "Arxigeas! Arxigeas! The best of the best! The best of the best!"

Arxigeena ran to him and gave him a huge hug. "Thank goodness you came!"

"Thank goodness Theotokis was here and told us about the rule! Thank you, Theotokis, and thank you, friends, for coming to help us!"

And they all went home happy.

But Kakeas, Orfeas... Kakeas will be back. This story isn't over...

And they all lived happily ever after... and we lived even better!
