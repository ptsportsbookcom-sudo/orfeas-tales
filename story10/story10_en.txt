The Birth of Rilaki

Once upon a time, three friends were inseparable — Theotokis, Aristotelis, and Rillas. Rillas was now married to Rilena, and one day she came up to him, glowing and excited. "Oh my God, Rillas, you won't believe it!" she said. "What happened? What won't I believe? Did crocodiles come and steal our bananas? Tell me!" "I'm telling you, you won't believe it," she laughed. "I'm so happy!" "Oh my God, something happened and you're teasing me now!" "Rillas, focus. Are you ready? I'm pregnant. We're going to have a baby!"

Rillas was thrilled beyond words. "Oh my goodness! That's wonderful! I love you so much, we're going to have a baby! We have to name it," he told Rilena. "I always wanted, if it's a boy, to name him Rilaki. If it's a girl, we'll call her Rilakena." "We don't know yet if it'll be a boy or a girl," Rilena told him. "Okay, we'll have the names ready either way," he answered.

A day passed, then two, and Rilena's belly began to grow. Rillas started to worry. "Oh no, what if something happens and my child isn't okay? I need to talk to someone! I'll go find Theotokis and Aristotelis and see what they think." He told Rilena he'd be gone for a bit. "Where are you going, Rillas? I'm pregnant!" "I'm going to find my friends, to tell them the good news." "Okay, okay."

So he set off for the city, jumping from tree to tree, grabbing a banana or two along the way. When he arrived, he couldn't remember which neighborhood his friends lived in. "Oh no," he thought, "if I keep doing this, the police are going to come round us up! What do I do now? I need to remember where Theotokis's house is." Then he remembered a landmark — a statue of a man pointing at the sky. "I'll go find that statue. But no one can see me, or they'll get scared."

He leapt from rooftop to rooftop, staying hidden, until he spotted the statue. He climbed down low. "Now which house is it? I have to call out!" "Theotoooooookis! Theotoooooookis!"

Theotokis happened to be home and heard a strange "psst, psst, psst." He went outside to see what was going on, but at first couldn't tell who was calling him. "Rillas, is that you?" he finally asked, recognizing his friend's voice. "Come inside, quick!"

Theotokis called Aristotelis right away, and once both boys were there, they asked Rillas what was wrong. "Guys, I have a big problem," he said. "What happened?" "Rilena's pregnant!" "Dude, that's the best news ever! Didn't you always want a baby?" "Yes, but now I'm really stressed out." "Why are you stressed?" "What if something goes wrong? What if something happens to Rilena?" "No way, don't think like that! Wait until the baby's born and we'll see. How do you know something will go wrong before it even happens?" "I don't know..." "Well then, why are you stressing out right now? Go home and take care of your wife. And if you need anything at all, we'll be right here for you." Theotokis handed him a phone. "Call us right away if anything happens," he told him. "Okay, okay."

Rillas went back to the jungle, and days passed, months passed. One day, Rilena suddenly told him: "I'm giving birth! I'm giving birth!" Rillas panicked at once and called Theotokis and Aristotelis on the phone to come help. They came running. Rilena started having contractions and the baby was on its way. Rillas looked over — and fainted! "Oh my God!" shouted Aristotelis. "Instead of Rilena being in pain, Rillas fainted! What is happening here?" They woke him up. "Are you okay?" "Yeah, I'm fine," he said. "I just got stressed and passed out."

A little later, Rilena called out again: "I'm giving birth! I'm giving birth!" Rillas looked again — and fainted a second time! "Oh my God," said Theotokis. They carried him to the living room and got him settled. "Sit here, you'll get to see Rilena in a moment." Finally, Rilena gave birth to a beautiful, perfectly healthy baby boy.

Rillas woke up, dazed. "Where am I? Where's Rilena? Where's the baby? Oh my God, panic!" Theotokis gave him a light tap on the face. "Focus! Focus!" he told him.

Then Rillas turned and saw his son. Little Rilaki! He was overjoyed. He hugged Rilena tightly. "Thank you," he told her. "Thank you forever, Rilena. I fainted twice..." Everyone laughed.

Rillas made a promise then: "I'm going to be the best dad in the world, and I'll always take care of you and Rilaki. And my two best friends are going to be his godfathers! I know we don't do baptisms, but they'll be his godfathers forever. Thank you, Theotokis and Aristotelis!"

And they all lived happily ever after, and we lived even better.
