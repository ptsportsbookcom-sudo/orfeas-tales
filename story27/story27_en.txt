The Ring in the Rock

Once upon a time, there was our friend Arxigeas. He had been going out with Arxigeena for a while now, and the two of them were having a wonderful time together. Then one day Arxigeas made up his mind.

"I want to marry this woman," he said. "That's it. Decided."

So he called his friends Theotokis and Aristotelis.

"Hey guys, I need your help," said Arxigeas. "I've decided to marry Arxigeena."

"Finally!" cried Aristotelis. "You've put our nerves at ease. Bravo!"

"Hey," said Theotokis, "chill, man. Chill. So what do you need from us?"

"I need ideas," said Arxigeas. "How should I propose to her?"

"You tell us your ideas first," said Aristotelis.

"I could take her for a ride in my spaceship and ask her up there."

"Boring!"

"I could hide the ring in a big bouquet of flowers."

"Boring, man!" said Theotokis. "What does she like? What is her very favourite thing?"

Arxigeas thought for a moment. "Her favourite thing in the world is rock climbing."

"Ah, I've got it!" said Arxigeas. "I'll propose to her on top of the highest mountain on Planet 650! But I want to do something really impressive. What do you think, guys?"

"Why don't you go there first," said Theotokis, "and hide the ring somewhere at the top? Then she can find it."

"Oh, what a great idea!" said Arxigeas. "I'm hanging up, guys. I'll keep you posted!"

So Arxigeas went and bought the most beautiful ring he could find. Then he flew in secret to Planet 650. There were no people there to bother him, so he parked his spaceship up in space and searched and searched for the highest mountain. At last he found one — an enormous mountain, and a very difficult climb.

He had also bought a special fake rock with a hollow inside that opened up. He put the ring inside and closed it. Once it was lying on the ground, nobody could tell — it looked just like any old rock. He placed it at the very top of the mountain. It would be the perfect surprise.

Days went by. One day Theotokis called him.

"We told Rillas and Maimudakis too," said Theotokis. "Everyone wants you to get married! What's happening?"

"Okay, okay, guys. I still have to arrange it," said Arxigeas. "But I've got the ring, I've got the rock — everything is ready."

"Well, we'll see!"

Many more days passed. Then Arxigeas said to himself, "Come on. Today is the day."

He called Arxigeena. "Hello, my love, how are you? Would you like to go rock climbing tomorrow?"

"Where?" she asked.

"On the most difficult mountain on Planet 650."

"Of course!" she said. "I love rock climbing!"

"Then get your clothes and your equipment ready," said Arxigeas. "We're going!"

The next morning, Arxigeas woke up very nervous. They climbed into the spaceship, and he put on his favourite love song to calm himself down. Arxigeena arrived, very happy.

"What's so funny?" asked Arxigeas.

"Ha ha, nothing!" she said. "I'm just excited that we're going climbing!"

They reached the mountain. It was huge, and the climb was very, very hard. Arxigeena was an excellent climber. Arxigeas was not — he was still learning.

Five minutes went by. Ten. Twenty. One hour. Two hours. Arxigeas was melting, and still they didn't stop! Finally he asked for a break. They were tied together with a rope, so they sat down on a ledge and talked for a while.

"Shall we keep going?"

"We keep going," said Arxigeena.

When they reached halfway, Arxigeas thought he was going to collapse.

"Oh my goodness," he puffed. "I have to push myself. I can do this."

Arxigeena looked at him. "Are you okay?"

"I'm fine! Yes, yes! We keep going!"

A little higher up, Arxigeas slipped! Luckily he was tied to the rope, but he slid all the way back down.

"Oh no," he groaned. "Halfway again! Oh my goodness!"

Somehow, after six or seven hours, they finally reached the top. Arxigeas was completely worn out. Arxigeena was overjoyed — she loved every minute of it. It had been worth all the effort.

Arxigeas looked down at the ground... and his heart sank. He couldn't remember which rock was the fake one with the ring inside! There were seven other rocks, all small, and all exactly the same.

He tried to remember. He got more and more nervous. He began to sweat.

Arxigeena looked at him. "Are you okay?"

"Yes, yes, I'm fine! Yes, yes!"

"Are you sure?"

"Yes, yes, yes! Er... shall we get back in the spaceship?"

Arxigeena laughed. She didn't say anything, but she could see how nervous he was.

Arxigeas picked up a rock and held it up. "Arxigeena, do you recognise this rock?"

"No," she said.

He tossed it away. "Give me a minute," he said. "Just one minute."

He picked up another rock and looked at it very, very closely. "Oh my," he thought. "This is it! This is my rock! I have to do it now — before she figures it out!"

So he got down on one knee, took Arxigeena's hand and said, "Would you like to open this rock and see what's inside?"

"Why?" she said. "Can rocks open?"

"You'll be amazed. Yes, they open! Go on, try it."

She opened it... "Ah! Ah! Ah!" She burst out laughing. "Are you teasing me? I knew it! I saw the rock and I could tell it was fake — and that you wanted to tell me something. So I decided to play along a little!"

"Oooh!" said Arxigeas. "You were teasing ME? I did so much preparation!"

"Well, okay," she laughed. "I just wanted to have a little fun."

Then Arxigeas opened the rock himself, got down on one knee again and said, "Arxigeena, will you be my wife?"

"Yes! Yes!" she said. "The way you planned it was beautiful. I teased you a little, but it was so romantic. I love you very much, Arxigeas."

"Oh, I love you very much too, Arxigeena."

A little later, as they were enjoying the view, the phone rang.

"Hey!" said Aristotelis. "What happened? Did she say yes or no?"

From behind Arxigeas, they heard Arxigeena laughing. "Ha ha ha! So you boys were in on it too! Hello, everyone!"

Everyone was thrilled. They all called to congratulate them, and everyone was so happy. And they planned the wedding right away: the wedding would take place on Planet 650, and then the big party on Aphrodite.

And they lived happily ever after — and we lived even better!

The End
