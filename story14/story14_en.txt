Rillas Goes to the Beach

One Sunday, Theotokis and Aristotelis decided to go to the beach. "Should we invite Rillas too?" asked Aristotelis. "Since he has Rilena and Rilaki, I don't think the whole family should come — a beach full of people isn't really for everyone." "Let's just invite Rillas, then. We'll ask if he wants a little break from the family." "Deal!"

They hopped on their quad bikes — VROOM VROOM VROOM! — and reached the jungle. "Rillas! Rillas!" Nothing. "Rilena!" Nothing. "Rilaki!" Nothing. "Where is everyone? Let's go check on Maimudakis."

They found Rillas and Maimudakis resting together. "Since we found you, want to come to the beach with us?" "The beach? But... everyone will see me!" worried Rillas. "Don't worry, we'll get you a hat and sunglasses, we'll figure it all out!" "Alright then! Will there be bananas?" "A hundred bananas!" "I just want some water," said Maimudakis. They planned to go the following Sunday.

They went to the supermarket and bought a hundred and one bananas, made sandwiches, packed water, and bought one-piece swimsuits for Rillas and Maimudakis, along with hats and sunglasses for their disguise.

On Sunday, they hopped on the quad bikes again — VROOM VROOM VROOM! — and rode to the jungle. Rilaki, Dakis, and Akis came running right away. "We want to come too!" "Kids, this trip is just for grown-ups," Rillas explained with a laugh. "Next time, I promise."

They arrived at the beach early and found a quiet spot with umbrellas. They dressed Rillas and Maimudakis in their swimsuits, hats, and sunglasses, and started eating their bananas.

Slowly the beach began to fill up with people. They ate, they laughed, and they dozed off for a bit. "I'm going for a swim!" said Aristotelis. "Me too!" said Maimudakis — but the moment his foot touched the water, he yanked it right back. "It's freezing! No thank you!"

"It can't be that bad," said Rillas, and waded into the sea. He loved it! He swam this way and that, until he reached the rocks and found crabs and little fish. "I'll dive under and pop up further down the beach — I'll surprise them!" he thought happily.

He dove deep — but forgot he was still wearing his hat and sunglasses! They slipped off into the water without him noticing.

When he surfaced, everyone saw him! "Help! A gorilla on the beach! Police!" the crowd screamed in a panic. Rillas looked around, confused: "What gorilla? Where?" "Aristotelis, the gorilla is right here, right in front of us!" shouted Theotokis. Rillas touched his face — no hat, no sunglasses. "Oh no! It's me — I'm the gorilla! Help, police!"

Rillas took off running, climbing trees, jumping fences, with everyone chasing after him. "Catch him! Call the zoo!"

He managed to hide. The police arrived and asked around, then questioned Maimudakis. "Sir, aren't you hot in all that?" "He's sick, don't get too close!" Aristotelis jumped in. "Kids, did you see a gorilla?" "He went that way!" they said, pointing in the wrong direction.

They searched everywhere but couldn't find him. Slowly it got dark, and the crowd began to leave.

The two friends went looking on their own — and found him fast asleep up a tree! "Wake up, Rillas! What happened? Did they catch us?" he asked, startled the second he opened his eyes. "No, no, relax — the police never found you, you just fell asleep!"

"Guys, I'm never going back to the sea!" said Rillas. "Why not?" "It's really nice, but there are way too many people, and I don't get why they were so scared — I never even talked to them! I was just eating bananas."

They rode home on the quad bikes. "How was it?" asked Rilena and Rilaki. "Oh, don't even ask..." They told them the whole story. "Last time!" said Rilena firmly. "Okay, okay," Rillas agreed. Theotokis and Aristotelis went home too. And they all lived happily ever after!
